01 / THE DEVELOPER

More than a server.
A whole ecosystem.

My work started with a simple idea: gaming communities deserve more than a place to log in. They deserve a connected home — with memorable worlds, tools that actually work, and people who want to stay.

That's the thinking behind Universal Gaming Network (UGN). I work across game servers, web applications, community tools, infrastructure, custom content, and ambitious browser-based experiences. I enjoy solving the difficult technical problems that sit between a big idea and something people can actually use.

I'm a hands-on, independent builder. I experiment, troubleshoot, redesign, and keep iterating. Some projects are live; others are actively evolving or still on the drawing board. This portfolio is a window into that process.

06 / QUICK QUESTIONS

A little more context.

What exactly is Universal Gaming Network?+

UGN is a gaming community and expanding ecosystem of Minecraft servers, community services, creative projects, and connected digital experiences. Its public home is universalgaming.us.

Can Java and Bedrock players join the Minecraft network?+

Cross-platform play is a core UGN goal, using technologies such as Velocity, Geyser, and Floodgate. Check the main UGN website for current server availability and connection details.

Are all these projects released?+

No. This portfolio intentionally separates established services, ongoing development, and planned concepts. A project appearing here does not mean that every feature described is available today.

What is the UGN philosophy on monetization?+

“Pay to Support the Server, Not Pay to Win the Game.” The aim is to fund development and operations through optional support and cosmetics, without selling unfair competitive advantages.

How can I follow the development journey?+

Explore the main UGN website, check the public GitHub organization, or join the community through Discord for news and conversations.
